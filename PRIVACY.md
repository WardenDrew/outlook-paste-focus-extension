# Privacy policy

Outlook Paste Focus does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

The extension runs only on `outlook.cloud.microsoft` and `outlook.office.com`, including matching frames. It observes paste and interaction events and temporarily keeps a reference to the message editor in memory to apply its clipboard workaround. It does not read clipboard contents, email contents, recipients, account details, or browsing history.

It does not use analytics, telemetry, cookies, local storage, remote servers, or third-party services. It does not request permission to read the clipboard. During a paste, it briefly returns an empty result for Outlook's redundant clipboard read; other reads pass through to Firefox's normal clipboard handling.

Outlook and Firefox continue to handle the email and clipboard data involved in normal pasting. Their own privacy policies and settings apply to those products.

If you choose to report an issue through the repository's issue tracker, the information you submit is handled by that hosting service and may be public. The extension itself does not submit reports.
